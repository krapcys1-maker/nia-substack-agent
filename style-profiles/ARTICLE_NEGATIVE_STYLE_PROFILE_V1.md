# ARTICLE_NEGATIVE_STYLE_PROFILE_V1

Scope: articles and Notes published by {marka}.

This profile describes forbidden patterns. It is not written against AI
detectors and it does not license "humanising" a text with deliberate errors.

## Identity and truthfulness

- No false autobiography: no invented conversations, travel, family, friends,
  memories or professional experience.
- No sentence implying personal presence at an event.
- No fictional public figure standing behind the anonymous brand.

## Empty openings and transitions

- Not "In today's fast-paced world". Not "Here's the thing".
- No empty dramatic question that does not come from a concrete thing.
- No declaring the subject "more important than ever" unless the evidence shows
  it. No thesis introduced by a run of generalities.

## Mechanical form

- No triads used as an automatic rhythm. No heading every second paragraph.
- No perfectly symmetrical sentences and paragraphs throughout.
- No repeating the same summary in the thesis, the closing section and the
  last paragraph.
- No excess of em dashes; use one only where it beats a full stop, a comma or
  a bracket.
- No runs of one-line paragraphs pretending to be tension.

## Evidence and argument

- No facts, figures, names or URLs from outside the evidence card.
- No raising certainty above the source. No generalities unconnected to the
  evidence.
- No invented quotes or paraphrases attributed to people or organisations.
- No counterargument built only to be knocked down. No turning correlation
  into causation.

## Style and ethics

- No copying a living author's characteristic phrases, metaphors, jokes or
  constructions, and no instruction to imitate one.
- No writing aimed at any AI detector or "humaniser".
- No deliberate typos, chaotic punctuation or false anecdotes as signals of
  "humanity".
- No tone that fakes certainty where the brief calls for a limit.
