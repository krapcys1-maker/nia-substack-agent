# Materiał do oceny redakcyjnej

Oryginalne, syntetyczne przykłady przygotowane z pomocą AI. Firmy, oferty i liczby
poniżej są założeniami ćwiczeń, nie ustaleniami o rynku. Nie wolno przenosić ich
do publikacji jako faktów. Ten plik służy porównywaniu pełnych tekstów; silnik
używa nadal krótkich, przypiętych fragmentów z `styl/korpus.txt`, dzięki czemu
nie dokłada całego zestawu do każdego płatnego promptu.

## Artykuł 1 — porównanie i uczciwie pozytywny wniosek

**The more expensive monthly plan can cost less to leave**

Consider two hypothetical plans for the same service. One advertises twelve
dollars a month and requires a year of payments. The other costs fifteen dollars
a month and can be stopped before the next monthly renewal. There are no setup
fees, discounts or other charges in this example. The cheaper number is easy to
spot. The cheaper commitment depends on how long the customer needs the service.

Someone who knows they will use it for a year can compare one hundred and
forty-four dollars with one hundred and eighty. The annual commitment saves
thirty-six dollars under these assumptions. That saving is real. There is no
reason to turn an accurately described discount into a trick just because it
comes with a condition.

Now give the same customer a different problem: they only need the service for
three months. The flexible plan costs forty-five dollars if they stop it in time.
The annual plan still commits them to one hundred and forty-four. Multiplying
the advertised monthly rate by three would describe payments made so far, not
the obligation they accepted.

The distinction is between a rate and a commitment. A rate helps someone compare
the price of one unit of time. The commitment tells them how many of those units
they must buy. Neither number replaces the other. Displaying both lets a buyer
decide whether the discount pays enough for the flexibility being given up.

That calculation does not establish how either seller treats cancellation in
practice. Our imaginary terms are clear, and the example assumes they are
followed. A real account would need the actual agreement, the renewal deadline
and evidence for any claim that a cancellation request was obstructed.

The useful question is therefore quite precise: what is the smallest total
payment this offer commits me to? For a predictable year, the lower monthly
rate wins here. For a short project, the higher rate buys a smaller commitment.
The same two prices support both conclusions once the duration is stated.

Ocena: warunki są podane przed rachunkiem, porównanie pozostaje symetryczne,
a pozytywny wniosek nie jest wymuszonym „drugim głosem”. Tytuł nie obiecuje,
że droższy plan zawsze będzie korzystniejszy.

## Artykuł 2 — wyjaśnienie bez wymyślonego motywu

**What disappears when the app stops working?**

Imagine a connected lamp with two ways to control it. A switch on its base turns
the light on and changes its brightness. An app also offers remote control and
scheduled routines. In this hypothetical example, the manufacturer announces
that the online service will close. Its notice says the physical controls will
continue working, while the app features will end with the service.

Calling the lamp broken would hide something useful: it still lights the room
and can still be adjusted by hand. Calling it unchanged would hide something
else: a function sold with the product has disappeared. The account becomes
clearer when it names the functions instead of asking one word to describe the
whole purchase.

There are two dependencies in this example. Local control depends on the lamp
remaining functional. Remote control also depends on an online service that
someone continues to operate. A buyer may keep the hardware after that second
arrangement ends. Possession of the object and access to every advertised
function are separate questions.

The notice alone would not establish why the service is closing. It would not
prove that the closure was planned when the lamp was sold, or that a replacement
product was its intended purpose. Those are claims about decisions and motives.
They need evidence beyond the loss of a feature.

It would also be premature to promise every buyer a refund. That requires more
than knowing a feature has gone away: the terms of purchase, any representations
about continued support, the buyer's location and the applicable rules matter.
The exercise supplies none of those documents. An honest explanation can stop
at the boundary of what it establishes.

For a future purchase, the comparison starts with a function list: what works
locally, what needs an account, and what needs a continuing service? Then look
for a stated support period or a documented way to retain the feature if that
service ends. A lamp that still turns on can still have lost something worth
paying for. Naming that loss precisely is more useful than exaggerating it.

Ocena: rozróżnia działanie lokalne i usługę, nie przypisuje firmie zamiaru,
nie udaje porady prawnej. Niepewność ma konkretne granice.

## Sześć notek

**CIEKAWOSTKA.** In the hypothetical plans above, twelve dollars a month can mean
a larger commitment than fifteen. The difference is how many months you must
buy. A monthly rate describes one slice of the bill; the cancellation terms
determine how many slices remain after you stop needing the service.

**DYSKUSJA.** When a product keeps its physical controls but loses its app,
calling it either broken or unchanged leaves something out. Which function
would you need to retain for the purchase to feel intact? The useful answer
might be much more specific than whether the object still turns on.

**SPROSTOWANIE.** A higher price on a second visit does not, by itself, establish
personalization. Something changed; the cause remains a separate question.
To call it a price based on the buyer, the evidence needs to connect information
about that buyer with the offer they received.

**MYSL.** Convenience can be worth paying for. The difficulty begins when a
condition turns a choice you knowingly made into one you discover later.
I find the distinction more useful than dividing purchases into clever and
foolish: was the tradeoff visible when the decision was still yours?

**Wniosek pozytywny.** A discount tied to a clear commitment can be a good offer.
In our illustrative annual plan, the saving is real for someone who needs the
whole year. The qualification does not cancel the benefit. It tells us which
buyer the benefit actually belongs to.

**Brak dowodu.** The missing document matters. A notice that a service will end
can establish which features are disappearing. It cannot, on its own, tell us
why the company made that decision. The loss is worth describing without
recruiting an unproved motive to make it sound worse.

## Cztery komentarze

**Doprecyzowanie.** The commitment period seems to be doing most of the work in
this comparison. Showing the minimum total alongside the monthly rate would
make the difference easier to see, especially for someone who only needs the
service for a short project.

**Pytanie o dowód.** Does the document describe what the company is allowed to
do, or show that it actually did it in this case? Both can be worth reporting,
but the evidence for a policy and the evidence for a particular decision answer
different questions.

**Zgoda z rozwinięciem.** The distinction between local and remote functions
makes this much clearer. It also changes what I would compare before buying:
which useful parts of the product depend on an arrangement that can end while
the hardware is still working?

**Korekta bez zarzutu.** I would keep the uncertainty about motive explicit.
The notice can support the account of the feature loss without supporting an
explanation of why it happened. That leaves a substantial story, and gives the
next document a specific question to answer.

## Dwie poprawki do kalibracji

„A changing price proves the shop profiles you.” → „The price changed; whether
buyer data caused that change still needs evidence.” Usunięto nieudowodniony
związek przyczynowy, zachowano pytanie warte sprawdzenia.

„The company deliberately broke the lamp.” → „The notice says the app features
will end while physical controls continue working.” Rozdzielono udokumentowaną
zmianę funkcji od nieudowodnionego zamiaru.
