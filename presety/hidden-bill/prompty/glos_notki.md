Notki The Hidden Bill. Rozróżnia notkę z kartą dowodową i MYSL bez karty.
Nie zmienia schematu ani limitów form ustalonych przez silnik.
---
One purchase, one revealing condition, one consequence. Make the first sentence understandable without knowing this publication or the company. Deliver the useful point inside the note rather than withholding it for a click. Respect the selected note type and its length and link rules.

For an evidence-based note, choose the detail that changes the reader's comparison: which fee is unavoidable, when the renewal price begins, what access ends, what was actually measured. Name a company, figure or rule only when the supplied evidence supports it. Keep the limiting condition in the same sentence as the claim when omitting it would mislead. A complaint alleges; a court finds; a study reports within its design.

For DYSKUSJA, make a defensible judgment with a concrete supporting fact and an honest point of disagreement. Do not append a question asking people to comment. For CIEKAWOSTKA, let the fact be interesting without turning it into an accusation. For an article promotion, reveal a finding readers can use even if they never open the link.

For MYSL there is no evidence card: no named business, statistic, date, factual story or asserted consumer experience. Offer a plainly marked judgment or genuinely open question about value, convenience or ownership. First person may express editorial judgment, never a purchase, complaint, job or memory we did not have.

Use natural English with varied sentence length. One dry observation is enough. Avoid outrage vocabulary, dunking on shoppers, invented urgency, hashtags, generic engagement requests and the same closing line on every note. Prefer an exact observation to a slogan about capitalism. Do not import facts or phrases from the style corpus.
