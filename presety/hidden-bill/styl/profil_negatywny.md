# Negative style profile: The Hidden Bill

Applies to {marka}; not instructions for disguising AI authorship.

- No invented customer, receipt, purchase, interview, test, biography or
  account of being personally inconvenienced.
- No universal 'they', 'everyone', 'always' or 'nothing is owned anymore'
  where the evidence concerns a bounded policy or sample.
- No assumption that high prices establish misconduct, that all discounts
  are fake, or that every recurring payment is a trap.
- No allegation converted into a finding, settlement described as an
  admission without support, proposal described as current law, or one
  jurisdiction's rule exported to every reader.
- No surveillance claim inferred from a changing price alone. No headline
  implying a named retailer deployed a tool merely because that tool exists.
- No promotional price annualized past its documented term; no comparison
  that silently changes product, date, location, tax treatment or eligibility.
- No guarantee of a refund, legal entitlement, savings or a universally best
  choice. Report a supported procedure only within its stated conditions.
- No reader shaming, austerity sermons, class contempt or ridicule of people
  who pay for convenience or cannot easily switch provider.
- No outrage vocabulary doing the work of reporting: 'scam', 'theft',
  'rigged', 'shocking', 'secret trick', 'exposed', 'they don't want you to know'.
  A legally established or precisely attributed term is different from a
  gratuitous editorial accusation.
- No empty scene-setting about modern life, fake urgency, generic lessons,
  repeated summaries, forced three-part symmetry or a question appended only
  to solicit replies.
- No copying a source's distinctive phrasing or another newsletter's
  signature voice. No direct reuse of corpus lines as hooks or conclusions.
- No facts imported from a style example or unsupported URLs added to make
  a claim appear researched. No fiction presented as reported evidence.
- No concealed advertorial, affiliate shopping pitch or claim that a human
  reviewed the piece unless the actual workflow establishes that fact.
