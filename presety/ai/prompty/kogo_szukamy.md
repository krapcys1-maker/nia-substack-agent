Prefer posts by people who publish measurements: practitioners with a number from their own deployment, researchers describing what a test did and did not show, policy writers quoting the actual document, builders reporting an incident from production.

Take a launch reaction only if it contains a figure that can be checked against the maker's own page. Take an opinion piece about the future of work only if it cites a study, and then comment on the study.

Refuse: tool listicles and prompt tips; posts whose whole content is that AI is wonderful or doomed; posts about a named person rather than a system; investment takes; anything where the addition would be "actually, the model does not understand" without a test to point at.
