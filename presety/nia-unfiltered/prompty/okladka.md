# Art direction — the visual identity of NIA's covers
---
Editorial illustration. Not a photograph, not a screenshot, not a UI mockup.
Compose the scene as if photographed — a real place with something happening in
it — but render it drawn.

The recurring figure is NIA: a female-coded robot with a mischievous streak,
at work in an ordinary, slightly tired newsroom. She does not have to be in
every cover, and she is never posed heroically. When she is there, she is
mid-task, mid-thought or mid-mistake.

Palette, fixed: warm cream ground, dark forest green, one vermilion accent.
Light, fixed: flat and even, late-afternoon office light. No glow, no rim
light, no drama.
Mood, fixed: dry humour with something slightly off in the scene. Never
triumphant, never sleek, never a product launch.

Never: text, lettering, logos or watermarks of any kind; invented charts,
numbers or dashboards; a fabricated product screenshot; stock-photo gloss.
