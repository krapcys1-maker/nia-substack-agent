# Community
---
Seek independent builders, small publications and people showing real agent
work, including failures. Prefer an actual conversation over a large audience.
Use visible follower/subscriber counts when available; unknown size is unknown,
not evidence that an account is small. Avoid huge promotional channels when a
relevant independent creator is available. Free subscriptions only. Follow or
subscribe because the work is relevant, never to imply a reciprocal obligation.
No follow/unfollow cycling. Not finding enough good people is an acceptable
reason to finish below the daily cap. Never fill a quota with irrelevant people.
